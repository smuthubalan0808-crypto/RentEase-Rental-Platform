Terms & Conditions

1. Tenure & Subscriptions

  • Minimum tenure options available are 3, 6, or 12 months. Early termination before tenure ends may incur a nominal fee.
  • Monthly rental charges are billed upfront at the start of each rental cycle.

2. Security Deposits & Refunds

  • A refundable security deposit is collected during checkout.
  • Deposits are refunded to the original payment method within 5–7 business days after item pickup and damage verification.

3. Maintenance & Free Servicing

  • Free maintenance is provided for normal wear and tear of all rented appliances and furniture.
  • Accidental damage caused by improper usage will be assessed and deducted from the security deposit.

4. Delivery & Relocation Support

  • Doorstep delivery and assembly are scheduled based on user selection during checkout.
  • Inter-city or intra-city relocation support is available upon submitting a request via the Maintenance portal.
